Placement Goals

Project Objectives

The overall aim of this project is to analyse sensor data generated during the manufacture of biosensor chips and investigate how manufacturing-stage measurements can support process monitoring and quality control.

Specific objectives:

1. Understand the biochip manufacturing process

   * Gain an understanding of the two-stage biochemical manufacturing workflow.
   * Identify the sensors used and the data generated during chip manufacture.

2. Explore and preprocess manufacturing sensor data

   * Review available datasets and data formats.
   * Clean, organise, and prepare time-series sensor data for analysis.

3. Develop feature extraction methods

   * Extract meaningful quantitative metrics from manufacturing sensor signals.
   * Identify features that characterise individual biochemical manufacturing steps.

4. Perform statistical analysis

   * Analyse feature distributions and variability across manufactured chips.
   * Identify trends, anomalies, and sources of manufacturing variation.
   * Propose statistical limits and metrics suitable for future quality control.

5. Investigate relationships with final biosensor performance

   * Compare manufacturing-stage features with final chip performance metrics.
   * Evaluate whether early manufacturing data can predict sensor accuracy, consistency, or overall quality.

6. Produce recommendations

   * Summarise findings and provide recommendations for future process monitoring, automation, and quality control procedures.
